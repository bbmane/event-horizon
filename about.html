what is this

Event Horizon is a community-curated calendar of upcoming sci-fi and cyberpunk releases: movies, series, anime, games, manga, comics, books and albums.

No ads, no tracking, and you don't need an account to add something. Every entry was suggested by a fan and approved by a moderator.

submit a release · report a correction · source on GitHub

credits

The submission form can autofill title, date and cover from:

  • TMDB (movies and TV series)
  • AniList (anime and manga)
  • Steam (video games)
  • Bandcamp (albums)

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Posters and covers belong to their respective owners and link back to the original source.
